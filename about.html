About Robem

A STEM and lab setup company built around classrooms, not catalogues.

Why we started Robem

Many schools own expensive lab equipment that stays locked in a cupboard because teachers are not trained to use it and no one plans how it fits the timetable.

Robem exists to fix that. We build the room, supply the kits, train the teachers and stay on to support the lab, so students actually use it every week.
